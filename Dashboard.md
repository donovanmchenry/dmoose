## Guardrails

- Analyze, organize, draft, and make reversible changes proactively.
- Require explicit approval before sending, publishing, purchasing, deleting, or
  acting on the user's behalf.
- Keep secrets and sensitive source material out of the repository.
- Mark uncertain claims and preserve their provenance.
